7-Day Return & Replacement Policy

At ComCare, we ensure 100% customer satisfaction for all physical product sales and hardware accessories.

1. Return Eligibility

You can request a return or replacement within 7 days of delivery for the following reasons:

  • Damaged product received during transit
  • Wrong item delivered
  • Defective or non-functional hardware
  • Missing parts or accessories

2. Photo Verification Requirement

Customers requesting a return must submit clear unboxing photos or damage pictures via the Customer Orders portal or WhatsApp (7782933700) for admin review.

3. Refund Processing

Once the returned product passes quality inspection at our Purnea warehouse, refunds are initiated within 48 hours to the original payment method or bank account.
